# 🦷 Elite Dental Clinic

### Modern Dental Care for a Healthier, Brighter Smile

🌐 **Live Website:** [www.elitedentalclinic.info](https://www.elitedentalclinic.info/)

## 📌 About the Project

Elite Dental Clinic is a modern dental clinic website designed to provide patients with information about dental treatments, clinic facilities, and consultation services. The website helps visitors explore available treatments, learn about the dental specialist, view patient experiences, and contact the clinic to book an appointment.

Located in Sirsa, Haryana, Elite Dental Clinic focuses on patient comfort, modern dental technology, and personalized dental care.

## ✨ Features

- **Home Page:** Introduction to the clinic and its dental care services.
- **Doctor Profile:** Information about Dr. Nandini Bansal and her expertise.
- **Dental Services:** Details of various general, cosmetic, and restorative dental treatments.
- **Appointment Booking:** Online consultation enquiry and appointment requests.
- **Patient Testimonials:** Patient experiences and reviews.
- **Before & After Gallery:** Visual presentation of dental treatment results.
- **Clinic Tour:** An overview of the clinic's facilities and environment.
- **Frequently Asked Questions:** Answers to common questions about dental treatments, consultation fees, and clinic visits.
- **Contact Information:** Clinic address, phone number, WhatsApp contact, and directions.
- **Responsive Design:** A website experience intended to help visitors access information across devices.
